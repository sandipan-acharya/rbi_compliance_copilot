24. Accounts opened using Aadhaar OTP based e-KYC, in non-face-to-face mode, are 
subject to the following conditions: 
(1) The Customer shall give specific consent for the authentication through OTP. 
(2) As a risk-mitigating measure for such accounts, the ARC shall ensure that it sends 
transaction alerts, OTP, etc., only to the mobile number of the customer registered 
with Aadhaar. The ARC shall have a Board- approved policy delineating a robust 
process of due diligence for dealing with requests for change of mobile number in such 
accounts.
