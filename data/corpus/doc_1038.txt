28. Simplified procedure for opening accounts by NBFCs:  In case a person who 
desires to open an account is not able to produce documents, as specified in paragraph 
23, NBFCs may at their discretion open accounts subject to the following conditions: 
(i) The NBFC shall obtain a self-attested photograph from the customer. 
(ii) The designated officer of the NBFC certifies under his signature that the person 
opening the account has affixed his signature or thumb impression in his presence.
