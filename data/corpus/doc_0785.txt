17. Weaker Sections 
17.1 Priority sector loans to the following borrowers will be considered as lending 
to Weaker Sections (overlapping category): 
 
(i) Small and Marginal Farmers 
(ii) Artisans, village and cottage industries where individual credit limits do not 
exceed ₹2 lakh  
(iii) Beneficiaries under Government Sponsored Schemes such as National 
Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), [****]19 and Self Employment Scheme for 
Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS) 
(iv) Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes 
(v) Beneficiaries of Differential Rate of Interest (DRI) scheme 
(vi) Self Help Groups/Joint Liability Groups 
(vii) Individuals and individual members of SHGs/JLGs [availing microfinance loans
