(v) The bank shall adopt a risk -based approach with respect to periodic updation of 
KYC. The bank shall clearly specify in its internal policy, duly approved by the Board 
of Directors of the bank or any committee of the Board to which power has been 
delegated, any additional and exceptional measures, it adopts that are not otherwise 
mandated under the above instructions, such as requirement of obtaining recent 
photograph, requirement of physical presence of the customer, requirement of periodic 
updation of KYC only in the branch of the bank where account is maintained, a more 
frequent periodicity of KYC updation than the minimum specified periodicity etc.  
(6) The bank shall advise the customers that in order to comply with the PML Rules, in 
case of any update in the documents submitted by the customer at the time of 
establishment of business relationship / account-based relationship and thereafter, as 
necessary; customers shall submit to the bank the update of such documents. This  
shall be done within 30 days of the update to the documents for the purpose of updating 
the records at the bank’s end.

letter, at appropriate intervals to its  customers through available communication 
options / channels for complying with the requirement of periodic updation of KYC.
