75. Issue and Payment of Demand Drafts, etc.: 
(1) Any remittance of funds by way of demand draft, mail / telegraphic transfer / NEFT 
/ IMPS or any other mode and issue of trave llers’ cheques for value of ₹50,000 and 
above shall be effected by debit to the customer’s account or against cheques and not 
against cash payment. 
(2) Further, the bank shall incorporate the name of the purchaser on the face of the 
demand draft, pay order, banker’s cheque, etc., by the issuing bank. These instructions 
shall take effect for such instruments issued on or after September 15, 2018.
