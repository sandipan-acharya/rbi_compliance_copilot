12. The b ank shall apply a Risk Based Approach (RBA) for mitigation and 
management of the risks (identified on its own or through national risk 
assessment) and shall have  policies, controls and procedures 2approved by the 
Board or committee of the Board to which pow ers have been delegated  in this 
regard. The bank shall implement a CDD programme, having regard to the ML / 
TF risks identified and the size of business. Further, the bank shall monitor the 
implementation of the controls and enhance them if necessary. 
13. Compliance of KYC policy: The bank shall ensure compliance with KYC Policy 
through:  
(1) specifying as to who constitute ‘Senior Management’ for the purpose of KYC 
compliance. 
(2) allocation of responsibility for effective implementation of policies and 
procedures. 
(3) independent evaluation of the compliance functions of the bank’s policies and 
procedures, including legal and regulatory requirements. 
(4) concurrent / internal audit system to verify compliance with KYC / AML policies 
and procedures. 
(5) submission of quarterly audit notes and compliance to the Audit Committee. 
14. The bank shall ensure that it does not outsource the decision- making functions 
of determining compliance with KYC norms.
