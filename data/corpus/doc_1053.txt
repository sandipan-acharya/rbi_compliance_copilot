48. The NBFC shall ensure that in case of customers who are non- profit 
organisations, the NBFC  registers details of such customers on the DARPAN 
Portal of NITI Aayog. If they are not registered, the NBFC shall register the details 
on the DARPAN Portal. The NBFC shall also maintain such registration records 
for a period of five years after the business relationship between the customer and 
the NBFC has ended or the account has been closed, whichever is later.
