B. CDD Measures for Sole Proprietary firms 
30. For opening an account in the name of a sole proprietary firm, the bank shall carry 
out the CDD of the individual (proprietor). 
31. In addition to the above, the bank shall also obtain any two of the following 
documents or the equivalent e-documents thereof as proof of business / activity in the 
name of the proprietary firm: 
(1) Registration certificate including Udyam Registration Certifi cate (URC) issued by 
the Government. 
(2) Certificate / licence issued by the municipal authorities under Shop and 
Establishment Act 
(3) Sales and income tax returns 
(4) CST / VAT / GST certificate 
(5) Certificate / registration document issued by Sales Tax / Service Tax / Professional
