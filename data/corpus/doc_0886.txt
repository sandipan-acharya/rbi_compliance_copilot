(3) The bank shall obtain the prior approval from senior management for 
establishing new correspondent banking relationships. However, the bank 
shall also take post facto- approval of the Board or the Committee 
empowered for this purpose. 
(4) The bank shall clearly document and understand the respective AML / CFT 
responsibilities of institutions involved. 
(5) In the case of payable-through-accounts, the correspondent bank shall be 
satisfied that the respondent bank has conducted CDD on the customer s 
having direct access to the accounts of the correspondent bank and is 
undertaking ongoing 'due diligence' on them. 
(6) The correspondent bank shall ensure that the respondent bank is able to 
provide the relevant CDD information immediately on request. 
(7) The bank shall not enter into or continue a correspondent relationship with 
a shell bank. 
(8) The bank shall ensure that the respondent banks do not permit their 
accounts to be used by shell banks. 
(9) The bank shall be cautious of correspondent banking relationships wi th 
institutions located in jurisdictions which have strategic deficiencies or have 
not made sufficient progress in implementation of FATF
