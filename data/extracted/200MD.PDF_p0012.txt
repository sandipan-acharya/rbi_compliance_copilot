Banks (UCBs) / State and Central Co- operative Banks (StCBs / 
CCBs), and any other entity which has been licensed under section 
22 of Banking Regulation Act, 1949, which as a group shall be referred 
as ‘banks’ 
(b) All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs) 
(c) All Non-Banking Finance Companies (NBFCs), Miscellaneous Non -
Banking Companies (MNBCs) and Residuary Non- Banking 
Companies (RNBCs) 
(d) Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs) 
(e) All Payment System Providers (PSPs) / System Partic ipants (SPs) 
and Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuers (PPI Issuers) 
(f) All authorised persons (APs) , including those who are agents of 
Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS), regulated by the Regulator. 
(xv) ‘Shell Bank’ means a bank that has no physical presence in the country in 
which it is incorporated and licensed, and which is unaffiliated with a 
regulated financial group that is subject to effective consolidated 
supervision. Physical presence means meaningful mind and management 
located within a country. The existence simply of a local agent or low -level 
staff does not constitute physical presence.
