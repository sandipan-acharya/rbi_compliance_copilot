(xix) ‘Small Account' means a savings account which is opened in terms 
of sub - rule (5) of rule 9 of the PML Rules, 2005. Details of the 
operation of a small account and controls to be exercised for such 
account are specified in paragraph 27. 
(xx) ‘Transaction’ means a purchase, sale, loan, pledge, gift, transfer, 
delivery or the arrangement thereof and includes: 
(a) opening of an account; 
(b) deposit, withdrawal, exchange or transfer of funds in whatever 
currency, whether in cash or by cheque, payment order or other 
instruments or by electronic or other non-physical means; 
(c) the use of a safety deposit box or any other form of safe deposit; 
(d) entering into any fiduciary relationship; 
(e) any payment made or received, in whole or in part, for any 
contractual or other legal obligation; or 
(f) establishing or creating a legal person or legal arrangement.
