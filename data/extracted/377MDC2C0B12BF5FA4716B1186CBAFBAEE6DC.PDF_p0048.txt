(6) The ARC shall upload KYC records pertaining to accounts of LEs opened on or

(7) Once KYC Identifier is generated by CKYCR, the ARC shall ensure that the same

(8) In order to ensure that all KYC records are incrementally uploaded on to CKYCR, 
the ARC shall upload / update the KYC data pertaining to accounts of indi vidual 
customers and LEs opened prior to the above-mentioned dates as per clauses (5) and 
(6), respectively, at the time of periodic updation as specified in paragraph 41 of these 
Directions, or earlier, when the updated KYC information is obtained / received from 
the customer. Also, whenever the ARC obtains additional or updated information from 
any customer as per clause ( 10) below in this paragraph or Rule 9 (1C) of the PML 
Rules, the ARC shall within seven days or within such period as may be notified by 
the Central Government, furnish the updated information to CKYCR, which shall 
update the KYC records of the existing customer in CKYCR. CKYCR shall 
thereafter inform electronically all the reporting entities who have dealt with the 
concerned customer regarding updation of KYC record of the said customer. Once 
CKYCR informs the ARC regarding an update in the KYC record of an existing 
customer, the ARC shall retrieve the updated KYC records from CKYCR and update 
the KYC record maintained by the ARC. 
(9) The ARC shall ensure that during periodic updation, the customers are migrated 
to the current CDD standard.
