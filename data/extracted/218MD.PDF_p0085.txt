appropriate department of the State/UT, administering the

individual/ entity visits or participates in any game in the Casino and/ 
or if any as sets of such designated individual/ entity is with the 
Casino operator, and of the particulars of any client matches with 
the particulars of designated individuals/ entities, the Casino owner

individual/entity approaches them, for entering/ investing in the
