10. The bank shall apply a Risk Based Approach (RBA) for mitigation and 
management of the risks (identified on its own or through national risk 
assessment) and shall have Board- approved policies, controls and procedures 
in this regard. The bank shall implement a CDD programme, having regard to 
the ML / TF risks identified and the size of business. Further, the bank shall 
monitor the implementation of the controls and enhance them if necessary. 
11. Compliance of KYC policy: The bank shall ensure compliance with KYC Policy 
through: 
(1) specifying as to who constitute ‘Senior Management’ for the purpose of 
KYC compliance. 
(2) allocation of responsibility for effective implementation of policies and 
procedures. 
(3) independent evaluation of the compliance functions of the bank’s policies 
and procedures, including legal and regulatory requirements. 
(4) concurrent / internal audit system to verify compliance with KYC / AML 
policies and procedures. 
(5) submission of quarterly audit notes and compliance to the Audit 
Committee. 
12. The bank shall ensure that it does not outsource the decision- making functions 
of determining compliance with KYC norms.
