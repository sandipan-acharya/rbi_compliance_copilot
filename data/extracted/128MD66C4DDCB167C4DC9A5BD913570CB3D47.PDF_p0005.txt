CATEGORIES AND TARGETS UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR 
 
5. Categories under Priority Sector 
 
The categories under priority sector are as follows: 
 
i. Agriculture 
ii. Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises 
iii. Export Credit 
iv. Education
