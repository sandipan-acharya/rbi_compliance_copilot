35. For opening an account of a trust, the bank shall obtain the certified copies of each 
of the following documents or the equivalent e-documents thereof: 
(1) Registration certificate 
(2) Trust deed 
(3) PAN or Form No.60 of the trust 
(4) Documents, as specified in paragraph 23, relating to beneficial owner, managers, 
officers or employees, as the case may be, holding an attorney to transact on its behalf 
(5) the names of the beneficiaries, trustees, settlor, protector, if any and authors of the 
trust 
(6) the address of the registered office of the trust; and 
(7) list of trustees and documents, as specified in paragraph 23, for those discharging 
the role as trustee and authorised to transact on behalf of the trust. 
36. For opening an account of an unincorporated association or a body of individuals,

equivalent e-documents thereof: 
(1) Resolution of the managing body of such association or body of individuals 
(2) PAN or Form No. 60 of the unincorporated association or a body of individuals
