24. Digital KYC Process: 
(1) The NBFC shall develop an application for digital KYC process and make it 
available at customer touch points for undertaking KYC of its customers and shal l 
undertake the KYC process only through this authenticated application. 
(2) The NBFC shall control the access to the Application and shall ensure that 
unauthorised persons do not use it . Authorised officials shall access the Application 
only through a login -id and password or a Live OTP or Time OTP controlled 
mechanism that the NBFC provides.
