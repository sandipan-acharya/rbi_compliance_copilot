Explanation: Transaction involving financing of the activities relating to terrorism 
includes transaction involving funds that the NBFC suspects are linked or related to, or 
to be used for terrorism, terrorist acts or by a terrorist, terrorist organisation or those 
who finance or are attempting to finance terrorism. 
(xix) ‘Transaction’ means a purchase, sale, loan, pledge, gift, transfer, delivery or 
the arrangement thereof and includes: 
(a) opening of an account; 
(b) deposit, withdrawal, exchange or transfer of funds in whatever currency, 
whether in cash or by cheque, payment order or other instruments or by electronic or 
other non-physical means; 
(c) the use of a safety deposit box or any other form of safe deposit; 
(d) entering into any fiduciary relationship; 
(e) any payment made or received, in whole or in part, for any contractual or other 
legal obligation; or 
(f) establishing or creating a legal person or legal arrangement.
