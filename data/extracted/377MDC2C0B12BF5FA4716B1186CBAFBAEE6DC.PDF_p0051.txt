64. Hiring of Employees and Employee training: 
(1) The ARC shall put in place an a dequate screening mechanism, including Know 
Your Employee / Staff policy, as an integral part of its personnel recruitment / hiring 
process. 
(2) The ARC shall endeavour to ensure that the staff dealing with / being deployed for 
KYC / AML / CFT matters have high integrity and ethical standards, good 
understanding of extant KYC / AML / CFT standards, effective communication skills 
and ability to keep up with the changing KYC / AML / CFT landscape, nationally and 
internationally. The ARC shall also strive to develop an environment which fosters 
open communication and high integrity amongst the staff. 
(3) The ARC shall put in place an on-going employee training programme so that the 
members of staff are adequately trained in KYC / AML / CFT policy. The focus of the 
training shall be different for frontline staff, compliance staff and staff dealing with new 
customers / acquisition of financial assets. The ARC shall specially train the front desk 
staff to handle issues arising from lack of customer educati on. The ARC shall ensure 
the proper staffing of the audit function with persons adequately trained and well -
versed in KYC / AML / CFT policies of the ARC, regulation and related issues.
